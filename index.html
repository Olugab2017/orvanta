Our Story

Orvanta Consulting & Advisory Ltd was founded with a clear conviction: that the gap between rigorous environmental science and practical decision-making in Nigeria is too wide, and too costly to ignore.

Operating from Sagamu, Nigeria, we are an environmental and hydrogeological consulting firm with a deliberate focus on evidence-based advisory, applied research, and technical capacity building. We work with institutions, funders, consultants, and public sector bodies who need environmental intelligence they can trust and act on.

The name Orvanta was chosen to reflect what we aspire to be — a firm with international standards, grounded in African knowledge, solving African challenges. We are registered in Nigeria (RC No. 9594969) and committed to operating with full professionalism, transparency, and intellectual rigour.

At Orvanta, we believe that good environmental science should not remain locked in academic journals. It should shape the decisions that affect communities, ecosystems, and development outcomes.

Flagship Research Initiative

ORVANTA RESEARCH SERIES · WORKING PAPER NO. 1

The Climate Vulnerability Amplification Index (CVAI)

How does soil contamination change the way communities experience climate risk? Current vulnerability indices — including ND-GAIN and standard IPCC frameworks — treat soil health as a background variable. The CVAI proposes that soil degradation and contamination systematically amplify climate vulnerability, and that this amplification effect can be measured, modelled, and incorporated into adaptation planning and funding decisions.

Developed under the Orvanta Research Initiative and grounded in evidence from oil-affected regions of the Niger Delta, the CVAI framework introduces an Amplification Factor (AF) scale designed to quantify how degraded earth systems magnify the effects of climate hazards on affected communities. This is open research — we are actively seeking collaboration, peer review, and co-development with institutions, researchers, and funders working in climate adaptation and environmental resilience.
